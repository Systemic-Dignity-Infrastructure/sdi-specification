# Zero-Trust Cybernetic Ledger

The governance of capital relies on a blind cybernetic ledger to prevent the creation of ghost assets. Oversight bypasses local administrators entirely. A global software technology team operates isolated from the physical structure. They monitor the deployment through live connectivity into each Asset Limited Modular Unit. 

The data arrives as a binary feed. The telemetry registers whether an ALMU draws electricity and cycles its digital door lock. The remote node determines occupancy strictly through physical physics. The tech team remains blind to resident identities. A local operator cannot falsify the electrical draw of a vacant room. 

The system achieves the total elimination of the ghost asset through automated physical compliance.
