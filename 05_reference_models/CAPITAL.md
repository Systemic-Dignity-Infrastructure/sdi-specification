# The Singular Prototype Gamble

The Singular Prototype requires a thirty-six million nine hundred thousand dollar capital injection. This figure operates as an economic hypothesis testing the minimum viable scale of the pipeline. The funding functions as a venture bet to prove the biological baseline. An independent financial reviewer with no stake in the project must verify this figure and its underlying methodology before any use in fundraising or capital procurement materials, as specified in the project governance pre-activation gates.

**Capital Derivation.** The thirty-six million nine hundred thousand dollar figure decomposes into three components: acquisition of 185,000 gross square feet of surplus commercial real estate (twenty-two million two hundred thousand dollars), fabrication and installation of three hundred forty-eight Asset-Limited Modular Units (eight million seven hundred thousand dollars), and structural hardening to residential occupancy standard — seismic retrofit, fire suppression and egress upgrades, and ADA compliance — (six million dollars). This breakdown is provisional pending the Independent Financial Review specified in GOVERNANCE.md §11.B, which must confirm at minimum: (1) whether the structural hardening line reflects current market seismic-zone retrofit costs for the target class of commercial tower, since six million dollars across 185,000 square feet (approximately thirty-two dollars per square foot) sits at the low end of typical combined seismic, fire, and ADA compliance costs for a commercial-to-residential occupancy reclassification; and (2) whether the prevailing wage premium mandated under AB 2011 (Constraint C-03) is already embedded in the acquisition and fabrication lines or represents an unbudgeted addition. No component of this figure is final until that review completes.

While the broader Systemic Dignity Infrastructure defines the biological, relational, and economic sequences necessary to reverse chronic homelessness, this document isolates the real estate mechanism. It translates the theoretical surplus real estate into an actionable financial structure designed for high-speed deployment in metropolitan cores. The initial deployment relies on private venture capital deliberately to bypass the need for municipal permission or taxpayer risk. The thirty-six million nine hundred thousand dollar injection isolates the prototype from political consensus. It absorbs the total risk profile of the experiment. 

The capital operates under a high-risk testing model designed to expose the true success and failure rate of the architecture. The deployment mandates open-source bookkeeping. Financial audits occur in real time against live biological occupancy data. 

This structural transparency ensures that the gamble produces verified open-source engineering data regardless of the final retention outcome. If the prototype produces undeniable physical data proving that unsheltered humans stabilize rapidly when granted fundamental privacy, the political objections evaporate. The architecture scales through verified physical momentum rather than rhetorical debate.

A single, independently evaluated Singular Prototype is mandatory before any network-scale multi-tower deployment proceeds. The prototype is the three-pod tower configuration — 348 residents, 76 permanent staff, 185,000 gross square feet — operated in partnership with an existing local operator possessing established clinical and residential capacity. Three pods is the minimum scale at which the fixed-cost amortization the architecture depends on can be observed and measured; a single pod does not generate the multi-pod staffing and cost data the specification requires. An independent third-party evaluator measures outcomes at ninety, one hundred eighty, and three hundred sixty-five days. The seven binary verification metrics constituting the Singular Prototype Threshold must pass before network expansion initiates.
